<h1>VetGo Privacy Policy</h1>
<p class="updated">Last updated: August 7, 2026</p>

<p>This privacy policy applies to the VetGo app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Nicolás Ansoleaga (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Commercial service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".</p>

<h2>Service Type</h2>
<p>VetGo is a practice management tool for veterinarians who provide in-home / mobile veterinary care. It has two types of users:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Veterinarians:</strong> Manage clients, patients, medical records, invoicing, and route planning for home visits. Some features may require a paid subscription for full access.</li>
  <li><strong>Clients (pet owners):</strong> Access a portal to view and manage their own pets' information. Basic use is free; clients may optionally make a one-time payment to unlock adding more pets than the free limit.</li>
</ul>
<p>VetGo does not display advertising of any kind, to either veterinarians or clients.</p>

<h2>Information Collection and Use</h2>
<p>The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:</p>
<ul>

  <li>The time spent on the Application</li>

<h2>Location Data</h2>
<p>VetGo requests access to location data specifically to support its core veterinary practice-management features:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Client and patient mapping:</strong> Veterinarians can view their clients' addresses on a map to plan which clients to visit.</li>
  <li><strong>Route planning for home visits:</strong> The app calculates travel distance, estimated time, and fuel/route cost between the veterinarian's current location and their clients' addresses, to help plan efficient visit schedules.</li>
  <li><strong>Radius search:</strong> Veterinarians can search for clients within a chosen distance of a location (their current position, or a point they select on the map), for example to identify and contact nearby clients about availability in a given area.</li>
  <li><strong>Business statistics by comuna/locality:</strong> Client addresses are used in aggregate, at the level of comuna (locality), to show veterinarians which areas their client base and consultations are concentrated in, helping them decide where to focus their work.</li>
</ul>
<p>Location data is only collected with your explicit consent, requested at the time the feature is used. You can allow precise location access, allow approximate location only, or disable location services entirely at any time through your device settings. If location access is denied, the features listed above will not be available, but the rest of the Application will continue to function normally.</p>

<p>The Application does not currently use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features. If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before any such processing begins.</p>

<p>The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.</p>
